We launched our SaaS MVP in 6 weeks with 2 people. Here's what we cut to make that happen:

❌ No custom design system. We used shadcn/ui and moved on.
❌ No admin panel. We edited the database directly for the first 3 months.
❌ No billing automation. The first 11 customers paid by invoice.
❌ No mobile app. Nobody asked for one.

What we didn't cut:
✅ Onboarding. We rewrote it 4 times.
✅ Talking to users. 3 calls a week, every week.

Result: 23 paying customers in the first 90 days and $4.1k MRR.

The hardest part wasn't building. It was saying no to features that felt important but weren't.

What did you cut from your MVP that you're glad you did?
