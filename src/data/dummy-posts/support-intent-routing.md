Hannah Moore
VP Customer Experience at Brightline Logistics
5d •

We cut first-response time by 38% this quarter without hiring a single person.

The change: we stopped routing tickets by channel and started routing by intent.

Before, every email went to the email queue and every chat went to the chat queue, no matter what the customer needed. Agents spent the first 3 minutes of every ticket figuring out what it was about.

Now an AI model reads each ticket, tags one of 12 intent categories and sends it to the agents who are strongest at that type of issue.

Biggest lesson: the AI model mattered much less than agreeing on those 12 categories first. We spent 3 weeks arguing about categories and 2 days setting up the model.

Next up: letting the model draft a first reply for the 4 most repetitive intents.
