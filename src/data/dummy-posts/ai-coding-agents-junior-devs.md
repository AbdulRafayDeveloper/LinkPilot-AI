Unpopular opinion: AI coding agents are making junior developers MORE valuable, not less.

We rolled out AI coding agents to our whole 18-person engineering team 4 months ago. Here's what actually happened:

→ Pull requests went up 41%.
→ Review time went up too, because someone still has to understand the code.
→ Our juniors who learned to write clear specs and small, testable tasks got the best results from the agents.
→ Two seniors who refused to use them are now our slowest reviewers.

The skill that matters most now isn't typing code. It's breaking a problem down so clearly that a machine (or a new teammate) can't misunderstand it.

We're changing our hiring loop because of this: less LeetCode, more "write a spec for this feature".

Curious if other teams are seeing the same thing.
