Last month we shipped an AI agent that drafts first replies to support tickets for a SaaS client.

It doesn't send anything on its own. It reads the ticket, pulls the 3 most similar solved tickets and writes a draft the agent can edit or throw away.

After 4 weeks: agents accept about 60% of drafts with small edits, and the team handles the same volume with one less person on the night shift.

The part that took longest wasn't the model. It was cleaning 2 years of messy ticket history so the agent had good examples to learn from.
