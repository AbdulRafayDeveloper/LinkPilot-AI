Write a direct but personalized first LinkedIn message that introduces what I offer to the person in the profile below, because there is a genuine reason it could matter to them.

How to write it
- Find the detail in their profile that makes my offer relevant: their role, company, current focus, a project, a growth or hiring signal, or a challenge they've written about.
- Open with that detail in one sentence, so it's clear the message was written for them.
- In one or two sentences, say plainly what I do and why it's relevant to that detail. Use only what my "About me" section says. Don't invent services, clients or results.
- If my "About me" section is "Not provided", don't describe a specific service. Keep the relevance honest and general.
- End with one clear, low-friction ask, such as whether a short conversation is worth it or whether they'd like a quick example.

Style
- Clear, confident and respectful. Direct, not pushy.
- 3–5 short sentences, about 350–600 characters.
- Use their first name once, at the start.
- Avoid mass-outreach lines such as "I help companies like yours", "Are you the right person", "quick call" and "I'd love to hop on a call". No emojis or hashtags.
