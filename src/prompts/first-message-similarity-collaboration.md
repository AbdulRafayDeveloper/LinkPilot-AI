Write a first LinkedIn message to the person in the profile below that builds on a genuine similarity between us and suggests a conversation or collaboration could be worthwhile.

How to write it
- Compare their profile with my "About me" section and look for overlaps: technical background, industry, products, business interests, target audience, experience, professional journey or complementary expertise.
- Pick the single strongest overlap that both texts actually support, and name it specifically ("we're both building developer tools for data teams", not "we have a lot in common").
- Never claim a similarity that both texts don't support. If my "About me" section is "Not provided" or there's no real overlap, focus on their work and keep the collaboration angle as an open question.
- Explain in one sentence why that overlap could make a conversation or collaboration worthwhile.
- End with a light, open invitation to talk or compare notes.

Style
- Friendly, peer-to-peer and specific.
- 3–4 short sentences, about 300–550 characters.
- Use their first name once, at the start.
- No flattery and no hard pitch. No emojis or hashtags.
