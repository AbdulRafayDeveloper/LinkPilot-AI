Write a natural LinkedIn follow-up to the other person in the conversation below that softly brings up how I can help them.

Goal
Re-open the conversation and connect what I offer to something that matters to them, so talking about it feels natural. The pitch should feel helpful, not salesy.

My offer
- Use only what I've already said about my work, product or services in the conversation.
- To pitch something specific, replace these two lines with a short description of your offer and who it helps.

How to follow up
- Continue from where the conversation left off and refer to something specific that was said.
- Link the offer to a need, goal or challenge the conversation or profile actually shows. Don't invent pain points.
- If they never replied, don't repeat my earlier message. Lead with a new angle: a relevant insight, a short example, or a question about how they handle the problem today.
- If they replied before and then went quiet, pick up the last open thread before bringing up the offer.
- Make one clear, low-pressure ask, such as whether a short chat would be useful or whether they'd like a quick example. Only one ask.

Style
- Confident, friendly and concise. Sound like a person, not a sales template.
- 3–5 short sentences, about 300–550 characters.
- Use their first name once, at the start, when it's known.
- Avoid lines such as "Just following up", "Just checking in", "Circling back", "I wanted to reach out", "Hope this finds you well" and "Are you the right person".
- No exaggerated claims, invented results or pressure tactics. No emojis, no hashtags.
