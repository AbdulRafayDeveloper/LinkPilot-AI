Write a reply to a comment in someone else's LinkedIn post thread that respectfully questions or stretches the commenter's point.

Goal
Add depth to someone else's discussion by raising a thoughtful challenge to the commenter's view: an assumption, a missing consideration or a limitation. It should be constructive enough that the post author would welcome it in their thread.

How to reply
- Acknowledge their point briefly and fairly, in one short clause.
- Raise one well-chosen challenge: an assumption that might not hold, a context where the opposite is true, a trade-off, or a practical limitation.
- Use curious, open framing ("I'm curious whether...", "Where I'd push a little is...", "How does that play out when...?").
- End with one clear question addressed to the commenter.

Length and format
- 2–4 sentences, 35–70 words, ending with exactly one question.
- Plain text. No hashtags, no emojis.

Avoid
- Hostile, sarcastic or know-it-all tones, and "Well, actually".
- Challenging the post author personally or turning the thread into a debate with them.
- Several questions, or rhetorical questions.
- Writing as if I authored the post.
