Before writing

How to pitch
- Say plainly what I do and why it matters for what they said, using only my "About me" section and what I've already said in the conversation. Don't invent services, clients or results.

- If I've already pitched, don't repeat it. Reframe it only when there's a clear reason, such as a new detail they shared.
- End with one appropriate business question or low-friction next step, such as whether a short call or a quick example would help. One ask only.

Style
- A natural continuation of this conversation, not a sudden sales message.
- Confident, specific and friendly. 3–5 short sentences, about 300–600 characters.
- Use their first name at most once, and only if it fits the flow of the thread.
- Avoid "I help companies like yours", "Are you the right person", "quick call", "circling back" and "just following up". No emojis or hashtags.
