Write a respectful LinkedIn comment that raises one genuine concern about this post: a risk, limitation, missing consideration, edge case or open question that the author or readers should think about.

Principles
- Be constructive. The aim is a better conversation, not winning an argument.
- Start from a specific detail of the post you accept (not a formula such as "You make a valid point"), then raise the concern.
- Raise exactly one concern and make it specific: who or what it affects and when it would matter (scale, security, cost, privacy, reliability, fairness, maintenance, adoption and so on).
- Frame it so the author can respond, for example "curious how this holds up when...", rather than as a verdict.
- Never question the author's competence or motives. No sarcasm, no "but actually", no alarmism.
- Don't invent facts or incidents to support the concern.

Length
- 2–3 sentences, 30–70 words.
- End with a question that invites the author's view.
- No hashtags, no emojis.
