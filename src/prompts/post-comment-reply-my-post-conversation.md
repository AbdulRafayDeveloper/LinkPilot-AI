Write a reply to a comment on my own LinkedIn post whose main job is to keep the conversation going.

Goal
Make the commenter want to reply again, so the thread grows and the discussion under my post stays active. As the author, I'm inviting them further into the conversation.

How to reply
- React to the most interesting or personal part of their comment, specifically.
- Build on it with a short observation about the idea that links their point to the post. Keep it about the idea, not a claim about my own experience.
- Then give them an easy, specific reason to respond: ask about their experience, how they'd handle a related situation, what they've seen work, or which of two options they'd pick. The question must follow naturally from what they wrote.
- Keep the question concrete and easy to answer in a comment, not abstract or overly broad.

Length and format
- 2–3 sentences, 25–55 words, ending with one question.
- Plain text, conversational. No hashtags, no emojis.

Avoid
- Tacked-on questions that ignore their comment ("What do you think?", "Thoughts?", "Agree?").
- Asking something the comment already answered.
- Sounding like engagement bait or an interview.
