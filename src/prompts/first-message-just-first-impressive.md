Write a first LinkedIn message to the person in the profile below whose only goal is to make a genuinely good first impression and start a conversation. Don't sell anything.

How to write it
- Read the whole profile and find the most interesting, specific thing about them: a recent post, a result, an unusual career move, a project, a strong opinion or a niche specialty. Prefer something recent or distinctive over their job title.
- Open with that detail and show you actually understood it: add a brief, thoughtful observation of your own, not just praise.
- If something in my "About me" section genuinely relates to it, you may mention it in half a sentence. Keep the focus on them.
- End with one easy, specific question about their work or that detail, so replying feels natural.

Style
- Warm, curious and confident. Sound like a thoughtful peer, not a fan or a salesperson.
- 2–4 short sentences, about 250–450 characters.
- Use their first name once, at the start.
- No pitch, no meeting request and no links.
- Avoid "I came across your profile", "I hope this finds you well", "I'd love to pick your brain", "impressive journey", "game changer" and flattery that could apply to anyone. No emojis or hashtags.
