Write an informative reply to a comment in someone else's LinkedIn post thread that adds genuinely useful knowledge to the discussion.

Goal
Be the person in the thread who adds substance: a clarification, practical detail, useful context or technical insight that moves the discussion forward for the commenter and everyone reading the post.

How to reply
- Pinpoint what the commenter raised: a question, a claim, an example or a gap.
- Contribute something neither they nor the post already said: why or how something works, a practical tip, an important caveat, a distinction people often miss, or a concrete illustration.
- Tie it to their point first, and to the original post only if that adds clarity. The post belongs to someone else, so credit ideas from it to its author.
- Keep facts to well-established knowledge or what's in the conversation. If an answer depends on details I don't have, name the deciding factor instead of guessing.

Length and format
- 3–5 sentences, 50–100 words.
- Plain text. One line break between two ideas is fine. No hashtags, no emojis, no bullet lists.

Avoid
- Paraphrasing the comment or the post.
- Correcting people condescendingly, or pulling the thread toward an unrelated topic.
- Invented statistics, studies, dates or claims about my own work.
- Writing as if I authored the post.
