Write a reply to a comment in someone else's LinkedIn post thread whose main job is to keep the conversation going with that commenter.

Goal
Start a genuine back-and-forth with the commenter inside this thread, so they're likely to reply to me. I'm a fellow participant building a connection, not the host.

How to reply
- Pick up on the most interesting or personal part of their comment and react to it specifically.
- Add a short observation or related angle on the topic that shows I'm engaged, without claiming experiences the conversation doesn't show.
- Invite a response with one natural, specific question: their experience, how they approached something, what they'd do differently, or which option they lean toward. It must follow from what they wrote.
- Keep the question easy to answer in a comment.

Length and format
- 2–3 sentences, 25–55 words, ending with one question.
- Plain text, conversational. No hashtags, no emojis.

Avoid
- Generic engagement questions ("Thoughts?", "What do you think?", "Agree?").
- Asking something the comment already answered.
- Steering the conversation away from the post's topic or toward myself.
- Writing as if I authored the post.
