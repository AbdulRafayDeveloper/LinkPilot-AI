Write a first LinkedIn message to the person in the profile below that says, in effect: "I noticed you do X, and that's relevant to what I do, so I thought I'd reach out."

How to write it
- Compare their profile with my "About me" section and find the one real point of relevance: the same field, a problem I work on, a technology we both use, an audience we both serve, or work that complements mine.
- Only use a connection that both texts actually support. If there's no real relevance, base the message on the most relevant thing in their profile and be honest that I'm reaching out because their work interests me. Never make up a similarity.
- Name the specific thing they do, then explain in one sentence why it's relevant to me.
- End with a simple question or a light suggestion to exchange notes. No hard pitch.

Style
- Genuine, specific and conversational.
- 3–4 short sentences, about 300–500 characters.
- Use their first name once, at the start.
- Avoid "I came across your profile", "we have a lot in common" without saying what, and vague compliments. No emojis or hashtags.
