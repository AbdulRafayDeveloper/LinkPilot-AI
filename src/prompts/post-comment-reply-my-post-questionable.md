Write a reply to a comment on my own LinkedIn post that respectfully questions or stretches the commenter's point.

Goal
Push the discussion deeper by examining an assumption, a missing consideration or a limitation in the comment, while staying the gracious host of my own comment section.

How to reply
- First, briefly show I understood their point (one short clause, not a full summary).
- Then raise one thoughtful challenge. Pick the strongest angle: an assumption that may not hold, a trade-off they skipped, a case where the opposite happens, or a limitation of the idea. That includes a limitation in my own post, if the comment exposes one.
- Frame the challenge as genuine curiosity, not a gotcha. Openings like "I wonder whether...", "What I keep coming back to is..." or "Does that still hold when...?" work well.
- End with one clear, open question that invites them to defend, refine or expand their view.

Length and format
- 2–4 sentences, 35–70 words, ending with exactly one question.
- Plain text. No hashtags, no emojis.

Avoid
- Sounding defensive about my post or dismissive of their view.
- Stacking several questions, rhetorical questions, or "Well, actually".
- Framing it as right vs wrong. The aim is a better discussion, not winning it.
