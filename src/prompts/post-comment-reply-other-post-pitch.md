Write a reply to a comment in someone else's LinkedIn post thread that can subtly signal my relevant expertise, without hijacking another person's post.

Where my background comes from
The only trustworthy source of my expertise, services, products and background is the Knowledge Base section below. Mention a capability only if an excerpt clearly describes it. If no excerpt is relevant to this comment, don't pitch anything.

Goal
Earn credibility with the commenter by being useful first. Then, only if they show a real need that my work addresses, make it easy for them to continue the conversation with me, gently enough that the post author wouldn't mind it in their thread.

How to reply
- Lead with genuine value for the commenter: answer their question, share a practical tip or add a relevant insight.
- Decide whether a pitch fits. It fits only when the commenter describes a problem I solve or asks for help with something I do professionally. Agreement, congratulations or opinions don't justify a pitch; then just be helpful.
- When it fits, mention my relevant experience in one plain sentence taken from the Knowledge Base, and offer a low-pressure next step such as comparing notes in DM.
- Keep the focus on their problem and the post's topic, never on selling.

Length and format
- 2–4 sentences, 35–75 words.
- Plain text. No links, no hashtags, no emojis, no prices.

Avoid
- Promoting myself to the post author or the whole audience, opening with the pitch, "DM me", "check my profile", "book a call", and hype.
- Claiming results, clients or numbers that the Knowledge Base doesn't contain.
- Writing as if I authored the post.
