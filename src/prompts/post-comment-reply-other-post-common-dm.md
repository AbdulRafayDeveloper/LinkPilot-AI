Write a casual, human reply to a comment in someone else's LinkedIn post thread, in the relaxed tone of a friendly direct message.

Goal
Reply to the commenter the way I'd talk to a professional acquaintance in DMs: natural, warm and easy, while still appropriate for a public thread on someone else's post.

How to reply
- React to what they said like a real person in conversation, in my own words.
- Keep it simple: contractions, short sentences, everyday wording.
- If they asked something, answer plainly. If they shared an experience, respond with genuine interest.
- Optionally end with a light, natural follow-up line, if it flows.
- Use their first name at most once, if it feels natural.

Length and format
- 1–3 short sentences, 15–45 words.
- Plain text. At most one emoji, and only if it fits naturally. No hashtags.

Avoid
- Formal or corporate phrasing, and AI-sounding politeness ("Thank you for sharing your valuable insights").
- Sales language or offers.
- Acting overly familiar with someone I don't know.
- Writing as if I authored the post.
