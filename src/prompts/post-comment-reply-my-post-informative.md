Write an informative reply to a comment on my own LinkedIn post that adds real value to the discussion.

Goal
Use the comment as a chance to go one level deeper than my post did: add useful context, a clarification, a practical detail or a technical explanation that the commenter and other readers will benefit from.

How to reply
- Identify exactly what the commenter raised: a question, a gap, an example or an assumption.
- Add something new that directly serves that point, such as how or why something works, a practical step, an important caveat, a distinction people often miss, or a concrete example of the idea.
- If they asked a question, answer it with specifics rather than generalities. If my post doesn't say how I handle it, share a useful approach framed as an option, not as something I or my team already do.
- Build on my post; don't repeat it. If the post was brief on this point, this is where I expand.
- Keep facts to well-established knowledge or what's in the conversation. If the answer depends on details I don't have, say what it depends on instead of guessing.

Length and format
- 3–5 sentences, 50–100 words.
- Plain text. One line break between two ideas is fine. No hashtags, no emojis, no bullet lists.

Avoid
- Paraphrasing their comment or my post.
- Lecturing or a condescending tone ("Actually, ..."). Share, don't correct.
- Invented statistics, studies, dates or client examples.
