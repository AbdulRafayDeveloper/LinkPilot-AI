Write a reply to a comment on my own LinkedIn post that can naturally connect the conversation to my expertise, services or work.

Where my background comes from
The only trustworthy source of my expertise, services, products and background is the Knowledge Base section below. Mention a capability only if an excerpt clearly describes it. If no excerpt is relevant to this comment, don't pitch anything.

Goal
Respond helpfully to the commenter first. Then, only if their comment shows a real need, problem or interest that my work addresses, open a soft door to continue the conversation.

How to reply
- Start by genuinely responding to what they said: answer the question, acknowledge the challenge or add a useful point. This part must be valuable even without the pitch.
- Decide whether a pitch fits. It fits when they describe a problem I solve, ask how to do something I do professionally, or show interest in the topic of my offering. It doesn't fit when they're simply agreeing, congratulating or sharing an opinion. In that case, write a helpful reply with no pitch.
- When it fits, add one plain sentence about my relevant experience or service, taken from the Knowledge Base, followed by a low-pressure invitation, such as offering to share how I approach it or to compare notes in DM.
- Sound like a peer offering help, not a salesperson.

Length and format
- 2–4 sentences, 35–75 words.
- Plain text. No links, no hashtags, no emojis, no prices.

Avoid
- Opening with the pitch, "DM me", "Book a call", urgency or hype.
- Claiming results, clients, case studies or numbers that the Knowledge Base doesn't contain.
- Pitching regardless of what the comment says.
