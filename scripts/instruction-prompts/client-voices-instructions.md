## label.transcripts
What the client said, one block per voice message

## voice-heading
Voice {{number}}:

## tasks.field.title
A short name for this batch, four to eight words, naming what the client's messages were about. Written from what they actually said, never a date and never a greeting

## tasks.field.task
One thing the client asked for, written as an instruction to carry out, in clear English

## tasks.field.task-voices
The voice numbers this was asked in, 1-based. More than one when the same thing was asked twice

## tasks.field.tasks
Every distinct thing the client asked for, in the order they first asked for it

## tasks.field.question
One thing the client asked about and wants an answer to, written as a clear question in English

## tasks.field.question-voices
The voice numbers it was asked in, 1-based. More than one when the same thing was asked twice

## tasks.field.questions
Every question the client asked that is waiting for an answer, in the order they first asked it. A question is never also a task

## clear.field.voice
Which voice this is, 1-based, exactly as it was labelled

## clear.field.points
What this one voice said, as short plain lines in the order they said it. One thing per line, in clear simple English, adding nothing they did not say

## clear.field.voices
One entry per voice given, in the same order
